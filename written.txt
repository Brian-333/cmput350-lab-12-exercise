Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?

The bounding boxes changes its sizes bounding the same circles that it initially bounds since the tree structure never changes.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?

The bounding boxes are rebuilt each time since the tree structure is updated. The bounding boxes will bound different circles according to the new tree.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?

There is no cutoff shown following the current instructions and orders.
But if scene.update() is changed after bvh.reset(), 
the BVH will not match the location since it's been moved and the tracing will not be accurate
and may be cutoff by the bounding box.
